Direct2Logbook ("we", "us", or "our") is a pilot logbook service operated under the Direct2Logbook name in Texas, United States. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and disclose information through direct2logbook.com and the Direct2Logbook mobile app. For privacy questions or requests, email admin@direct2logbook.com.

Information You Provide

  • Account information: your name, username, email address, company if provided, account preferences, and authentication information. Passwords set with Direct2Logbook are stored as password hashes.
  • Logbook information: flight dates, routes and airports, aircraft types and registrations, flight times, operating rules, landings, approaches, remarks, and other records you enter or import. We use these records to provide your logbook, maps, totals, recency information, and exports.
  • Signatures: when you draw or update a signature, we store a representation of it for display in your account and logbook PDF exports.
  • Communications: information you send when requesting support or contacting us.

Medical Certificate Expiry

We store your medical certificate class, issue date, and age-category selection to calculate and display your medical certificate's expiration date. The age-category selection is used only to calculate medical certificate expiry. This feature does not ask for your date of birth, diagnoses, medical examination results, or medical history. You can review and update these settings in your profile.

Please avoid including medical diagnoses, payment-card details, or other unnecessary sensitive information in remarks or support messages.

Google Sign-In

If you choose Google sign-in, Google provides your Google account identifier, name, email address, email-verification information, and basic profile information, which may include a profile-picture URL. We store linked-account information to create your account, authenticate you, and connect an existing account when you request it. Your name and email are also used for the account, trial, billing, and service communications described in this policy.

We request profile and email permissions, not access to your Gmail messages, contacts, calendar, or Google Drive files. We do not receive your Google password. You can manage linked accounts in the website's account connection settings or revoke access through your Google Account. You may need another sign-in method before disconnecting Google. Disconnecting Google does not delete your logbook, close your Direct2Logbook account, or cancel a subscription. Google's own handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Payments and Subscriptions

Stripe handles payment-card entry and payment processing. We send Stripe your name and email address to create a customer record and provision a trial or subscription. This can happen when your trial starts, before you enter payment details. We store Stripe customer and subscription identifiers, subscription status, billing-period dates, and records needed to reconcile billing events. Direct2Logbook does not store your full payment-card number or card security code.

Stripe processes information to provide payment services and for its own fraud-prevention, security, and legal-compliance purposes, as explained in the Stripe Privacy Policy.

Technical Information and Cookies

Our servers and service providers receive technical information such as IP addresses, browser or device details, access times, requested pages, and error information. We use this information to operate the service, investigate problems, protect accounts, and prevent abuse.

The website uses cookies and related storage for sign-in sessions, security, and preferences. Google reCAPTCHA helps protect registration from automated abuse and may process device and interaction information. Our public landing page includes Google Analytics code. When Google scripts, fonts, maps, or other externally hosted resources load, their providers receive technical request information and may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies.

You can control cookies through your browser, although blocking them may prevent sign-in or other features from working. The service does not currently change its behavior in response to browser "Do Not Track" signals. Third-party services may collect information about your activity across websites or apps under their own policies. This does not limit rights you have under applicable law.

Mobile Storage and Synchronization

The mobile app stores downloaded records, account information, preferences, and pending changes on your device to support offline use. Changes made offline are sent to our server when the app synchronizes, including automatically when connectivity returns. Signing out does not by itself delete your server account or necessarily erase locally saved records.

You can use your device's app-data controls to remove local data. Unsynchronized changes may be lost if you clear app data or uninstall the app. Removing local data does not delete server records or cancel your subscription. Maps use the routes in your logbook; the current app does not record your movements using background GPS tracking.

Service Providers and Other Disclosures

We use providers to operate the service. Depending on the feature you use, information is processed as follows:

  • Hosting and backups: DigitalOcean hosts the application and database. Backblaze B2 stores off-server database backups, which are encrypted before upload. See the DigitalOcean privacy policy and Backblaze privacy information.
  • Email delivery: Mailgun processes recipient addresses, message contents, attachments such as requested logbook PDFs, and delivery information to send service emails. See the Mailgun privacy policy.
  • Error monitoring: Sentry receives diagnostic reports to help us find and fix problems. Reports can include account identifiers, IP addresses, browser or device details, error messages, and request context. See the Sentry privacy policy.
  • Authentication, payments, and abuse prevention: Google and Stripe process information for the purposes described above.
  • Maps and website resources: OpenStreetMap supplies website map tiles; the mobile app uses Apple or Google maps depending on the platform. Map providers receive technical requests and information about the map area displayed. Providers of fonts and other website resources also receive technical requests. See the OpenStreetMap privacy policy, Apple privacy policy, and Google Privacy Policy.

Providers process information under their applicable terms and privacy policies; some also have independent purposes, such as preventing fraud or complying with law. We do not sell or rent your logbook records, medical-certificate settings, or signature to advertisers or data brokers, or use those records to target advertisements. We do not sell, rent, or lease our customer lists.

We may also disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to respond to lawful requests, protect accounts and the service, investigate fraud or abuse, or protect people's rights and safety.

Service Emails

We send account verification, welcome, account-linking, security, billing, and other service messages, as well as exports you request. These messages support the features you use. A request to stop promotional communications does not stop necessary account or security messages. Contact us about communication preferences at admin@direct2logbook.com.

Retention and Backups

We retain account and logbook information to provide your service. Canceling a paid subscription does not automatically erase your logbook. You can delete individual records through the service or request account deletion using the contact details below.

Retention also depends on the purpose of the information: for example, resolving synchronization and billing issues, investigating security incidents, handling disputes, meeting legal obligations, and recovering from data loss. Billing, security, support, and operational records may need to remain after account deletion for these purposes. Providers may retain records for their own legal obligations.

Deleting a record from the active database does not immediately remove it from existing backups. Our scheduled backup rotation normally removes archives older than 30 days while preserving at least seven verified backups. Backups may remain longer when that minimum has not been met or recovery or legal obligations require it. Backup deletion requests are handled subject to applicable law.

Your Choices and Privacy Requests

You can review and correct account, medical-certificate, and logbook information using the available account and editing screens, and request a logbook PDF. To request access to or a copy of your personal information, correction, account deletion, or assistance exercising a privacy right, email admin@direct2logbook.com. Include the email address associated with your account and what you are requesting; do not send passwords, payment-card details, or unnecessary sensitive documents.

We may ask for information reasonably needed to verify your identity and protect your account. Available rights depend on your location and applicable law and may include access, correction, portability, deletion, withdrawal of consent where processing relies on consent, and objection to or restriction of certain processing. We respond within applicable legal time limits. If we cannot fulfill a request, we explain the reason; you can ask us to review that decision by replying to the response. You may also complain to an appropriate privacy regulator where that right applies. We do not penalize you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

Security and Processing Locations

We use safeguards including HTTPS for connections to our service, account access controls, password hashing, and encryption of off-server database backups. No system or transmission is completely secure. Protect your account credentials and device, especially when it contains offline logbook records.

We operate from the United States. Our providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate, whose privacy laws may differ from those in your location. Contact us for information about processing locations and any transfer safeguards applicable to your information.

Children

Direct2Logbook is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Children under 13 should not create an account or submit personal information. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate steps to remove it.
